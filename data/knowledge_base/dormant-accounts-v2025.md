# Dormant Accounts Procedure v2025

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff identify and reactivate dormant accounts.

## §2 When an account becomes dormant
An account becomes dormant after 12 months with no customer-initiated transactions.

## §3 Reactivation
To reactivate a dormant account, verify the customer's ID and ask the customer to make a deposit or withdrawal at the counter.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=deceased|death|estate|passed away) Dormant accounts of deceased customers must be **referred to Estate Services**.
- [C2] (when: mentions=joint) For a joint account, **all account holders** must be present to reactivate it.
