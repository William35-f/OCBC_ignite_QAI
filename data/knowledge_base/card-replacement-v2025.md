# Card Replacement Procedure v2025

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff replace a lost, stolen or damaged debit card.

## §2 Lost or stolen card
Block the card immediately in the card system, then raise a replacement request.

## §3 Replacement fee
A replacement fee of S$15 is charged to the customer's account.

## §4 Delivery
The replacement card is mailed to the customer's registered address.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=stolen|theft|police) If the card was stolen, advise the customer to **make a police report**.
- [C2] (when: mentions=damaged|faulty|fault|broken) The **replacement fee is waived** for cards damaged by a manufacturing fault.
