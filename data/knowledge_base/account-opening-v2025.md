# Account Opening Procedure v2025

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff open a personal savings account.

## §2 Identity documents
Citizens and permanent residents must show their national identity card. Foreigners must show a passport and a valid employment or student pass.

## §3 Initial deposit
The minimum initial deposit for a savings account is S$500.

## §4 Account activation
The account is activated once the identity documents are verified and the initial deposit is received.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=minor|child|under 16|teenager) Customers under 16 must open a **joint account with a parent or guardian**.
- [C2] (when: mentions=foreigner|foreign|passport; residency=foreigner) Foreigners also need **proof of a local residential address**.
