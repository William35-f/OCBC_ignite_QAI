# Address Change Procedure v2024

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff update a customer's mailing address.

## §2 Documents
The customer must provide photo ID and a proof of address dated within the last 3 months.

## §3 Updating the record
Update the address in the core banking system and mail a confirmation letter to the new address.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=overseas|abroad|foreign address) An overseas address needs a **utility bill or bank statement** as proof.
