# Fixed Deposit Early Withdrawal Procedure v2025

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff handle a request to withdraw a fixed deposit before maturity. It replaces the v2022 procedure.

## §2 Early withdrawal interest
Interest of 0.05% p.a. is paid on the amount withdrawn before maturity.

## §3 Partial withdrawal
Partial withdrawal is allowed in multiples of S$1,000.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=deceased|death|estate|passed away) For a deceased customer's deposit, **refer the case to Estate Services**.
- [C2] (when: mentions=partial) A partial withdrawal must leave a **balance of at least S$1,000** in the deposit.
