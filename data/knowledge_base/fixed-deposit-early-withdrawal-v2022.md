# Fixed Deposit Early Withdrawal Procedure v2022

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff handle a request to withdraw a fixed deposit before maturity.

## §2 Early withdrawal interest
No interest is paid on a fixed deposit that is withdrawn before maturity.

## §3 Partial withdrawal
Partial withdrawal of a fixed deposit is not allowed.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=deceased|death|estate|passed away) For a deceased customer's deposit, **refer the case to Estate Services**.
