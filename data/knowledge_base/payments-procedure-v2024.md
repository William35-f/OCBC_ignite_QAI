# Payments Procedure v2024

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff process outward overseas transfers for retail customers.

## §2 Scope
It applies to telegraphic transfers sent from a customer's savings or current account at a branch counter.

## §3 Limits

### §3.1 Overseas transfer limit
Overseas transfer limit: S$10,000 per day for all payees.

## §4 Before processing

### §4.1 Identity verification
Before processing, verify the customer's ID against the account record.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=sanction|sanctioned|embargo) Transfers to **sanctioned countries** must not be processed; refer them to Compliance.
