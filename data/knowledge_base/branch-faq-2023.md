# Branch FAQ 2023 – Overseas transfers

## §1 Who can send an overseas transfer?
Any customer with an active savings or current account can send an overseas transfer at a branch counter.

## §2 What is the limit for a new payee?
The overseas transfer limit for a new payee is S$10,000 per day.

## §3 Can a customer cancel a transfer?
A transfer can be cancelled at the counter only before it has been processed.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=cancel|cancellation|recall) Cancellations after processing must be **referred to Payments Operations**.
