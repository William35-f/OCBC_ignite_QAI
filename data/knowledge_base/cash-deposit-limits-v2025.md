# Cash Deposit Procedure v2025

## §1 Purpose
This procedure explains how branch staff accept cash deposits at the counter.

## §2 Counter deposits
Cash deposits below S$20,000 per transaction can be accepted without additional checks.

## §3 Large deposits
Cash deposits of S$20,000 or more require a source-of-funds declaration form.

## §4 Deposit machines
Cash deposit machines accept up to 100 notes per transaction.

## Conditions and exceptions
- [C1] (when: mentions=on behalf|third party|someone else|another person) Deposits made on behalf of another person require the **depositor's ID**.
